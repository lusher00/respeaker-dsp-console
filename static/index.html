Calibrate mic spacing

Stand directly off one END of the board, in line with both mics (that is ±90°), and make broadband noise — hiss, rub fabric, crinkle paper — for the whole window. The largest consistent delay is the spacing. Doing it from the right side also confirms channel order: the result should say right.
